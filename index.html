About Course Road Map

What is Course Road Map?

Course Road Map is a comprehensive learning platform designed to help students from various engineering departments master web development and programming skills.

Our platform provides a structured learning path, starting from the basics (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) and progressing to advanced topics like React, Flask, and Git version control.

Why Choose Us?

  • ✅ Structured Learning Path: Follow a proven roadmap from beginner to advanced
  • ✅ Progress Tracking: Keep track of your learning progress with our built-in system
  • ✅ Multiple Departments: Courses tailored for CSE, IT, ECE, EEE, and AI & DS students
  • ✅ Free Access: All courses are 100% free to access
  • ✅ Interactive Roadmap: Visual learning paths make it easy to understand course prerequisites
  • ✅ Search & Filter: Find exactly what you're looking for with powerful search and filter features

Our Mission

To empower engineering students with practical web development and programming skills through a structured, interactive, and accessible learning platform. We believe in learning by doing and providing a clear roadmap for success.
